  • Source: NIH RePORTER, fiscal years 2022–26, NIH institutes and offices only (other HHS agencies and NIH's own intramural research are left out). FY2026 is still being revised: NIH publishes its final records for a year about three months after it ends.
  • Counting: each award's total cost (direct plus indirect) in the fiscal year NIH records it, as NIH reports it. A multi-project grant counts once, at its parent's total. Federal Award Insights counts NIH obligations by date from USAspending, so its NIH figures differ slightly (about 2% for most institutions).
  • Recipients: the same crosswalk and rules as Federal Award Insights. Hospitals are left out unless the university claims them in NSF's HERD survey (such as Cincinnati Children's with the University of Cincinnati), so universities whose research runs largely through independent hospitals show less here.
  • New, renewed and continuing: from NIH's application type: new competing awards, competing renewals, non-competing continuations of multi-year awards, and supplements.
  • Programs are an institute and an activity code together, such as NCI's P30 center grants, so a peer comparison shows mechanisms at specific institutes. A program counts as held with any counted award in FY2022–26.
  • Departments are as NIH records them; about 30% of NIH dollars carry no department.
  • No individuals: the site shows institutions, institutes, award types and departments, never researchers' names. Each award links to its NIH RePORTER page.
  • Other sources: Federal Award Insights and NSF's HERD survey count differently, so their figures differ. Why figures differ between sources.
